Udhar khata

Your udhar register, without the paper

Every shop gives udhar. Stockly keeps the khata for you, so nobody argues about what is owed and nothing is forgotten.

  • Sell on udhar from its own screen, with the same products. All on udhar, or part paid now and the rest on the khata.
  • A register for every customer: each udhar sale, payment and return with the date, who served, and a running balance.
  • Receive payments by cash, Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank, part or full.
  • WhatsApp reminder in one tap, with the amount already written in.
  • Returns come off the khata first; only what they no longer owe is paid back in cash.
  • Udhar limit per customer. A cashier cannot go past it; the owner can.
  • Bring over your paper register: enter what each customer already owes.
  • See who is late: anyone who has paid nothing in 30 days is flagged.
  • Cash in the drawer in the day's report, kept separate from udhar, so the till count matches.
  • The receipt shows paid now, added to khata, and the new balance.
Daily backups

If the computer breaks, your records don't

A shop's records are worth more than the computer they sit on. Stockly copies them every day, by itself.

  • Every day at the times you choose (9 PM to start), a full copy is saved on the computer. The last 7 are kept.
  • Connect Google Drive once and every copy is also uploaded to a Stockly backups folder in your own Drive.
  • Stockly sees only that folder, nothing else in your Drive, and your Google sign-in is stored encrypted.
  • Computer was off at backup time? It backs up soon after it starts.
  • A warning on every screen if there has been no backup for 3 days.
  • Back up now and Download backup whenever you like, and simple steps to restore.

The security warning on first run

Every program downloaded from the internet gets a warning the first time. This is normal. Here is what to click.

Windows

  1. Double-click Start Stockly.
  2. If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. If "Open File - Security Warning" appears, click Run.
  4. Say yes to installing Node.js when asked. Stockly runs on it.

Mac

  1. Right-click Start Stockly and choose Open.
  2. Click Open again in the warning. You only do this once.
  3. Enter your Mac's password when it installs Node.js.

Questions

Does it need internet?

Only for the install. After that, selling, stock, receipts and reports all work with no internet at all.

Where is my data kept?

In one file on your own computer, inside the Stockly folder. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The only copy that leaves the computer is the backup you choose to keep in your own Google Drive.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, in the shop or from home. On the Phone access page, connect your own free ngrok account once and Stockly gives you a secure link and a QR code. Scan it on each phone, sign in, and add it to the home screen. The shop computer needs to be on with internet; your records stay on it.

How does udhar work?

Open Udhar khata and press Sell on udhar. Add items as usual, choose the customer (or add a new one with a name and mobile), then choose all on udhar or part paid now. The amount goes on their khata. When they pay, open their name and press Receive payment.

Does an udhar sale reduce my stock?

Yes, at the moment of sale, exactly like a cash sale: the goods have left the shop. A return puts the stock back. Receiving a payment later only reduces what the customer owes.

Can a customer return something they took on udhar?

Yes, from their khata: press Return next to the sale. The refund comes off what they owe first; anything beyond that is paid back in cash, and Stockly shows the split before you confirm.

What happens if my computer breaks?

Stockly backs up every day. Connect Google Drive once on the Backups page and every copy is also kept in your own Drive. On a new computer, install Stockly, put the backup in place, and every product, sale and khata comes back.

Can my cashier see my profit?

No. Cashiers can sell and take returns. Profit, cost prices, stock editing and expenses are for the owner only.

What if I change computers?

Copy your data folder to the new machine and message us with the computer code shown on screen. We issue a new key for the new computer at no charge.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay Rs 30,000 once for the licence. Optional yearly support is available if you want someone on call.

I forgot my password.

It can be reset on the shop computer. The install guide shows how, and we can walk you through it on WhatsApp.
